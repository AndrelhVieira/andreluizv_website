Hi, I'm **André Luiz Vieira**, a Frontend and Mobile Engineer from Brazil, now moving to **Dublin, Ireland**.

For the past five years I've been building React and React Native products for banks, credit unions and e-commerce. I care about the parts users feel and teams live with: fast screens, accessible interfaces and code the next developer can read without a tour.

## What I do

I work at [DB1 Global Software](https://www.db1.com.br/), a Brazilian software company that builds products for clients in finance, retail and other industries.

- I led the development of a **React Native e-commerce app with 100,000+ downloads** on Google Play. The squad was still being formed, so I also defined the architecture, the code standards and how we shipped releases.
- I build web features with **React, Next.js and TypeScript** for core banking platforms used by **50,000+ customers**.
- I review code, track delivery metrics and spend a good part of my week aligning scope and trade-offs with product and UX.
